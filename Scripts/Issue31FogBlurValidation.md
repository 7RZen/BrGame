# GitHub #31 — More blurring

## Decisions / implementation
- Developer-only `bFogDimBlur=True`, `FogDimBlurRadius=25 cm` (runtime/config
  clamp 0–40 cm after P2 review), and existing `FogEdgeFeather=60 cm` (0–150 cm).
  DimOpacity remains 0.6 and FogDimDelay remains 2 s.
- Main-view M_Fog only, before DOF. No minimap/full-map material changes,
  replicated gameplay changes, player options, plugins or module dependencies.
- 16 golden-angle disk taps for inward-only world-XY boundary averaging;
  center visible/known coverage gates prevent outward dilation. Point center
  mask gates additionally prevent bilinear coverage from exposing unknown
  center texels. Timestamps remain point-sampled.
- At most 16 content taps, projected from world-cm XY offsets, then reconstructed
  from each tap's actual scene depth with UE 5.8's perspective/ortho helpers.
  Point scene-color taps require their own point known mask plus bilinear
  coverage weight. Reject off-view/off-map taps, depth surfaces more than 10 cm
  above/below the center, and actual XY displacement outside the radius (+1 cm
  raster tolerance). This avoids borrowing foreground wall/raised-cover pixels
  using a floor mask. No sampling of an occluded surface behind scene depth.
- P2 review fix: after accepting an endpoint, sample quarter/half/three-quarter
  points on its center→tap viewport segment. Each probe reconstructs world Z
  from its actual point-sampled depth texel. Reject a rise over the existing
  10 cm threshold, a depth-matched stencil 1–17 surface, or an unknown strip.
  Rejection happens before endpoint scene color is sampled. At most 48
  intermediate probes per dim pixel, with early rejection; no additional
  color kernel taps. C++ and HLSL both cap content radius at 40 cm.
- Depth-matched stencil 1 and 2–17 centers return the original sharp color and
  existing obstacle light calculation; those surfaces cannot be content taps.
  Unknown centers skip both kernels; fully visible interiors skip content blur
  after boundary filtering. All currently visible centers skip content blur.
- Content blend uses exactly the same `saturate((Time-stamp)/Delay)` as dimming,
  independently of boundary coverage. Missing tap support retains center color
  via a center-weighted normalized kernel, not dark/undefined samples.
- Generator checks UE 5.8 `recompile_material` returned errors, preserves asset
  paths and only changes zone-ring pass if needed (avoids unrelated resaves).
- Fog smoke now explicitly selects Town/TestArena instead of trusting a saved
  machine-local host map preference.

## P2 thin-wall review follow-up (2026-11-07)

### Safety cap / residual assumption
- Content radius default stays 25 cm; maximum is now **40 cm**, enforced by
  UPROPERTY metadata, the tested pure config clamp, and an HLSL clamp for
  manually supplied MIDs. Larger old config values are safely bounded without
  a config/save migration. `DefaultGame.ini` documents the cap.
- Three intermediate probes split a nominal planar, orthographic world segment
  into four gaps of at most 10 cm at the cap (6.25 cm at defaults), before the
  existing +1 cm endpoint raster tolerance. This is a **bounded screen-space
  approximation, not an arbitrary-thickness wall guarantee**.
- Map-authoring assumption: a blocker rises >10 cm above the sampled surface
  (or has depth-matched obstacle stencil) and its rasterized projected span
  covers a probe. A conservative planar top-down span must exceed one probe
  gap plus depth-texel/world reconstruction uncertainty. Subpixel walls,
  narrower spans between probes, depth-invisible/translucent blockers and more
  oblique/perspective projections can still be missed. The 40 cm cap limits
  this risk; it does not make screen-space sampling equivalent to ray tracing.
  Test each supported zoom/camera/map; reduce radius or disable content blur
  for maps violating this assumption. Town's 40 cm walls exceed the default
  radius, but that is not relied on as the only check.
- Edge softness remains bounded at **150 cm**, default 60 cm. It samples fog
  masks only, never scene color, and multiplies center LOS/known gates, so
  cross-wall color transport does not apply. It may darken farther inward at
  larger values, but cannot expand exploration or LOS.
