## Design choices
- **Vision vs low cover:** Low cover (110 cm) uses the `BrLowCover` profile. It blocks movement and bullets (WorldStatic) but ignores BrVision, so it does not block fog vision. Full walls (280 cm), containers and trunks use the BlockAll profile, so they block vision and bullets. Roads and floors are NoCollision. Vision traces start 20 cm above the origin, so floors never block vision.
- **Trade-off:** grenade blasts trace WorldStatic/WorldDynamic (`Source/BrGame/Combat/BrGrenade.cpp:139-148`) and BrLowCover is WorldStatic. So low cover **does** stop grenade blast line of sight, and bullets are blocked too. Low cover only passes fog vision. This is an accepted trade-off.
- **Wall height:** full walls are 280 cm. The camera is orthographic, so taller walls would hide the area behind them at the 45° pitch the user set. This is not solved with a complex mechanism; see Open items.
- **Spawns:** player starts are at Z=100. The respawn candidate is start+4 cm with a capsule of r=42, half-height 96. A start at Z=50 made every candidate overlap the ground, so respawns failed. This was the cause of the Grouped failure and is fixed.
- **Boundary:** invisible blocking walls at ±10100 cm (hidden in game).
- **Navmesh:** the NavMeshBoundsVolume covers ±10200 cm. The commandlet cannot tick, so no build is forced; the game builds it at runtime. The PIE world sets `ENavigationBuildLock::NoUpdateInPIE` (flag 0x4), which makes `Build()` a silent no-op. The test removes that lock with `RemoveNavigationBuildLock(..., Rebuild)`, then calls `Build()`, so the navmesh really builds (see Validation results). The lock only applies in PIE. Standalone `-game` runs are not lock-blocked.
- **Doorways:** every intended entrance is at least 120 cm clear (standing capsule r=60). No partition or furniture sits in a doorway. This is enforced by the generator audit and by the `BrGame.Map.TownLayout` sweep test.

## Spawn / pickup / zone / fog-LOS checks
- Spawns: `BrGame.Map.TownLayout` checks that each PlayerStart's respawn capsule (r=42, hh=96, ECC_Pawn) does not overlap blocking geometry. 48 starts pass, and all are inside the playable bounds.
- Pickups: `BrGame.Map.TownLayout` checks that all 339 pickup points are inside `ABrZoneBounds::FindPlayableBounds`, that a WorldStatic sphere (r=30, lifted 40) overlaps nothing, and that each point projects onto the navmesh. Flow logs show `Pickups spawned: Count=32 Slots=32` at 2 players (24 + 4·players).
- Zone: the `-Zone` flow smoke completes all stages, so the target circles chosen are reachable. Reachability is not asserted directly in the test.
- Fog LOS: `BrGame.Map.TownLayout` picks one isolated probe per blocker category (building wall, container, trunk, map boundary, low-cover crate) and traces `UBrVisibilitySubsystem::VisionChannel` across it. For each probe it asserts the component's BrVision response (Block for walls/containers/trunks/boundary, Ignore for low cover) and the number of vision blockers on the crossing (exactly one for blocking categories, which must be the probed actor; zero for low cover). A probe is considered found only when its actor is isolated. The test traces the channel directly, because `UBrVisibilitySubsystem::LOS` requires a character.
- Navmesh: the PIE world (`UEDPIE_*_L_Town`) gets its lock released and a runtime build (`RecastNavMesh-Default`, 1.02 s). The test waits with `IsNavigationBeingBuiltOrLocked` and `IsNavigationBuildInProgress`, with a 120 s elapsed-time timeout and a 1 s settle, then proves reachability with `FindPathSync` (min-X to max-X pickup, 9 points).

## Manual playtest checklist
1. Host a game, pick a mode, and press Start. The lobby should travel to L_Town.
2. Spawn and confirm you are not stuck in a wall, and that you can move away from the spawn.
3. Walk through a building doorway (150 cm gap) and check that the walls block bullets and movement.
4. Shoot through low cover and confirm it blocks bullets; confirm enemies behind low cover are still visible.
5. Enter a building and check that walls block fog vision from outside.
6. Walk to the map edge and confirm the boundary blocks movement.
7. Watch the zone shrink through several stages and confirm the rings draw correctly.
8. Pick up items and confirm they respawn.
9. Respawn after a death and check you don't spawn inside enemies or walls.
10. Run `-MatchLevel Lvl_TopDown` once to confirm the template map still works.

## Open items (design follow-ups, not blockers)

Screenshots show more density, but the compound still reads fairly open and sight lines remain long (report-only metric: compound mean ~1465 cm, forest mean ~1265 cm). The placement saturates its attempt cap at the current `DENSE_GAP=130`, so more density needs a layout-level change (e.g., more walled sites or bigger site footprints), not a higher target. Playtest decision needed. Fog is out of scope for this change.
- **Dense-zone fog rendering** is a separate open item (other agent); fog black regions cover much of the screenshots.
- **Tall-wall occlusion at 65° pitch.** Full walls at 280 cm did not visibly hide large areas in the captures. A playtest with 30 players is still the real check.

- **Dev-only screenshot hook.** `BrShotSmoke.cpp` is compiled out of shipping builds. It can stay, or be removed later.
- **Launcher leaves processes.** `RunLocalMultiplayer.ps1` leaves ListenServer and Client1 running by design. Stop them by PID when done.
