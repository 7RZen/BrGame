# GitHub issue 20 - crate quests and round score

## Decisions

- Each landed crate adds 1-2 separate quest tokens among its scattered loot. Duplicate offers are allowed; the first successful E interaction consumes the offer. Offers not accepted expire with ordinary crate loot.
- Players may have multiple accepted quests. Objectives are down an opponent, collect a hand grenade, or collect a specified weapon; weapon target is selected when the offer spawns. A down completes the down objective.
- Informational round score: +1 for a credited final elimination, -1 for each down, another -1 for the later death/respawn or final elimination, and +2 per completed quest. Down/death penalties stack; score does not affect the winner.
- Accepted quests and score persist for a reconnect and reset with the round.

## Implementation

- Quest offers are server-created pickup payloads; acceptance is authoritative, one-use across players, and quest progress follows successful pickup application / actual down transitions.
- Per-player quest journal and signed score live in the round ledger and restore exactly on reconnect. Final-eliminator attribution is held across an unresolved down episode and cleared on revive/respawn.
- HUD summarizes duplicate objectives without merging their independent completion rewards. Results show the score without changing winner evaluation. Zone text has a separate HUD column from the quest summary.
- The quest pickup DataAsset is generated by `Scripts/Content/create_quest_pickup_asset.py` and referenced by developer config. Session protocol was bumped for the replicated schema change.
